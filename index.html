QUICK GUIDE

Read every projection clearly

Separate and togetherPoints, rebounds and assists each have their own model. PRA is those three added up, so the combined number always matches the parts.
80% rangeBuilt from the model's real past misses on similar projections. About 8 in 10 actual results should land inside it.
MinutesProjected playing time. It drives everything else, so a player listed Questionable or on a back-to-back can swing a lot.
Opponent rankHow much the opponent allows to this position group lately, per minute. No. 1 is the toughest matchup on tonight's slate.
Injury tagsFrom the league injury report. Players listed Out are removed. Doubtful players stay on the board but are left out of the top lists.
ReliabilityHigher means a tighter historical range and steadier recent minutes. It is not a probability.

VALIDATION

Model health

Month-by-month walk-forward testing, not random splitting.

Each month is predicted by a model trained only on games before it, so no game ever helps predict itself. Every feature uses information available before tip-off. Ranges come from those walk-forward errors.
